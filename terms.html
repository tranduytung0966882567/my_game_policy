How to Cancel & Manage Subscriptions

You have full control over your subscriptions. You can cancel, downgrade, or manage auto-renewal at any time. When you cancel, you will continue to enjoy VIP benefits until the end of your current paid billing period.

On iOS (iPhone / iPad):

  1. Open the Settings app on your Apple device.
  2. Tap your Name / Apple ID banner at the top.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Select Games Hub VIP Pass.
  5. Tap Cancel Subscription and confirm.

You can also manage your subscriptions directly via Apple's web portal: https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.

On Android:

  1. Open the Google Play Store.
  2. Tap your profile icon at the top right.
  3. Tap Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.
  4. Select Games Hub and tap Cancel Subscription.
