Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Games Hub mobile application (referred to as "we", "us", or "our"), available on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.

By downloading, accessing, or playing Games Hub, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please discontinue using the application.

02

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal identifying information (such as your legal name, physical address, email address, or phone number) unless you contact us directly for customer support. The information we process falls into three categories:

A. Device & Diagnostic Information (Automated)

When you play Games Hub, our diagnostic and analytics SDKs may automatically collect:

  • Device model, manufacturer, and operating system version (iOS/iPadOS or Android).
  • System language, locale, and timezone settings.
  • Anonymous advertising identifier (Apple IDFA or Google Advertising ID GAID), subject to your tracking permission.
  • App performance, frame rate metrics, crash logs, and diagnostics via Firebase Crashlytics.

B. Gameplay & Offline Progress Data

All your high scores, level completions (e.g. Knife Hit, 2048, Nut Sort, Caro, Block Puzzle), audio preferences, and remaining play passes are stored locally on your device using secure local storage (SharedPreferences / Key-Value Store). We do not transmit or sell your offline game scores to third parties.

C. In-App Purchase & Subscription Records

When you purchase or restore the Game Hub VIP Pass, transactions are handled exclusively by Apple App Store (StoreKit) or Google Play Billing. We only receive anonymous cryptographic receipt tokens to verify active VIP status. We NEVER receive, process, or store your credit card numbers, bank information, or Apple ID / Google account passwords.

03

How We Use Your Information

We use the processed data solely for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Deliver Core Features: Allow you to play offline and 2-player mini-games, save game progress, and unlock VIP games.
  • Validate VIP Access: Verify active subscription receipts on iOS and Android to provide an ad-free experience and unlimited plays.
  • App Performance & Crash Fixing: Monitor crash reports via Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose bugs and improve 60-120 FPS performance.
  • Advertising (For Non-VIP Users): Serve non-intrusive banner and interstitial advertisements via Google AdMob to keep the free version accessible.

In-App Subscriptions & Auto-Renewal

⭐ Apple App Store & Google Play Subscription Terms

Games Hub offers an optional auto-renewable subscription: Game Hub VIP Pass (1 Month).

• Payment: Payment is charged to your Apple ID or Google Play Account upon confirmation of purchase.
• Auto-Renewal: Subscription automatically renews every month unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing cycle.
• Account Renewal Charge: Your account will be charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period.
• Managing Subscriptions: You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in your device Account Settings (Apple Subscription Management or Google Play Subscriptions).

Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Games Hub is designed for general audiences and casual game lovers of all ages. We do not knowingly collect, request, or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).

If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately. We will promptly delete such information from our records.

07

Your Privacy Rights & Data Deletion

Depending on your location (including the European Economic Area under GDPR, and California under CCPA/CPRA), you have rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Reset / Delete Local Data: You can delete all your stored scores and local app cache at any time simply by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.
  • Right to Opt Out of Tracking: On iOS, you can decline tracking permission via the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt or go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Right to Request Data Deletion: To request deletion of any diagnostic logs associated with your advertising identifier, email us at the contact address below.
